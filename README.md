# Roast Calculator

Welcome to **Roast Calculator**! This interactive web app helps you calculate the optimal cooking time for your roast based on several factors, including the weight, desired doneness, and temperature units.

## Features

- **Roast Weight Input**: Specify the weight of your roast (in kilograms or pounds).
- **Doneness Level**: Choose your preferred doneness level (Rare, Medium Rare, Medium, Medium Well, Well Done).
- **Temperature Units**: Select your preferred temperature unit (Celsius or Fahrenheit).
- **Responsive Design**: The app is optimized for both desktop and mobile devices.
- **User-Friendly Interface**: A clean, cozy, and visually appealing design that blends seamlessly with the experience of cooking.
- **Results Display**: After inputting your values, get precise cooking time recommendations and target temperatures.

## How It Works

1. **Enter the weight of your roast**: Specify the weight in either kilograms or pounds.
2. **Choose your desired doneness level**: Select the doneness you want for your roast (e.g., Rare, Medium).
3. **Select your temperature units**: Choose whether you'd like to work in Celsius or Fahrenheit.
4. **Press "Cook!"**: The app will calculate the cooking time and display the target and pull temperatures for your roast.
5. **View the results**: The results are displayed in a clean, easy-to-read box below the form.

## Installation

To run the app locally, clone the repo, and then:

```bash
npx http-server
```

Navigate to `http://localhost:8080`
